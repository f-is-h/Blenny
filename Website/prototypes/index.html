Six ways in.

Rough website directions for Blenny. Each one is a mood sketch, not a finished page. Open one, play with it, and pick the direction worth building.

01 · SCROLL STORY

Descent

The page is a dive. Icons fall out of a crowded menu bar and settle at their depth: sunlight, twilight, midnight. A live depth gauge and a WebGL sea.

Try: scroll down, then click the fish in the menu bar.
01B · SCROLL STORY · REVISED

Descent B

Descent rescaled to where blennies really live: a 0–60 m gauge with the blenny's range marked. Surface, Reef and Deep map to Visible, Revealable and Hidden; revealable icons move into crevices like blennies do.

Try: scroll down, then click the fish in the menu bar.
02 · INTERACTIVE HERO

Night Dive

Pitch dark. Your cursor is a torch lighting a wet reef in real time. Hidden things only show their eyeshine, and they hide from your light.

Try: move around, then press and hold.
02B · INTERACTIVE HERO · REVISED

Shallows

Night Dive moved up to 12 m, where blennies actually live. Sunlit water and sand, a reef wall in shade, and a shaft of sunlight you steer, with caustics and real self-shadowing. Shade-loving eyes duck when the sun finds them.

Try: steer the sunlight, then press and hold.
03 · PHYSICS TOY

Waterline

The menu bar floats on the sea. Drag icons off it and drop them on the reef or into the deep. The product explains itself by being played with.

Try: drag icons from the bar, then tap the fish.
04 · WILDCARD

Sonar

A phosphor-green submarine console. Your menu bar as sonar contacts on three depth rings, with a typed contact log and a real ping.

Try: press P (sound on) and R.
